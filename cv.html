Curriculum vitae

Carlos Arocha, FSA, SCR

I am a Zurich-based consulting actuary, educator, and founder of Arocha & Associates. My work combines actuarial depth with climate risk, data science, and model governance to help insurers and other organizations turn complex technical questions into decisions that can be explained, governed, and implemented.

I work across insurance and reinsurance, drawing on international experience in pricing, reserving, financial reporting, solvency, quantitative risk modeling, machine learning, and professional education.
